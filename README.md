# 🌿 Thryve — Student Mental Wellness & Stress Management

**Thryve** is a web-based student mental wellness application designed to help students understand their stress levels, track their daily emotions, and access helpful wellness resources. It provides a supportive digital space where students can reflect on their emotional well-being and receive motivational guidance to manage academic pressure and everyday stress.

The application features an interactive stress assessment, a daily mood tracker with a personal journaling interface, motivational messages, and student support resources. With a modern, visually appealing interface featuring pastel gradients, glassmorphism effects, and light and dark themes, Thryve aims to make mental wellness more accessible and engaging for students.

## ✨ Features

- **🔐 User Authentication:** Register, log in, and log out using a username and password.
- **🧠 Stress Assessment:** Evaluate stress levels based on sleep duration, mood, and family pressure.
- **📊 Stress Analysis:** Calculate a score and categorize the result into Low, Moderate, or High Stress, with corresponding advice.
- **😊 Daily Mood Tracker:** Record daily moods and write personal journal entries.
- **💬 Motivational Messages:** Display randomly selected inspirational quotes to encourage a positive mindset.
- **🧘 Wellness & Support Resources:** Access practical stress-management tips and support information.
- **🎨 Modern UI:** Enjoy a clean interface with pastel gradients, glassmorphism-style cards, glowing effects, and interactive buttons.
- **🌙 Light & Dark Themes:** Switch between light and dark visual modes.
- **⚙️ Settings & Help:** Access application settings and guidance on using its features.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Backend:** Python, Flask
- **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript
- **Database:** SQLite
- **Templating Engine:** Jinja2
- **Deployment Server Dependency:** Gunicorn

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. Create an account and log in to the application.
2. Open the assessment section and provide the requested details.
3. Answer questions about sleep, mood, and family pressure.
4. View your calculated stress score, stress category, personalized guidance, and a motivational quote.
5. Use the mood tracker to record emotions and write journal entries.
6. Explore support resources and settings for a more personalized experience.

## 🎯 Project Objective

The primary objective of Thryve is to encourage students to pay attention to their emotional well-being by providing simple self-reflection tools, stress awareness features, motivational content, and accessible support resources in one application.

**Note:** Thryve is intended for general wellness and self-reflection. Its stress assessment is a simple, rule-based scoring mechanism, not a clinical diagnosis or substitute for professional mental health care.
